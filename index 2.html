Terms & Consent
ClaimGuard is an independent AI-powered information service that helps homeowners understand and navigate property insurance claims. By using ClaimGuard, you understand and agree:

• ClaimGuard provides AI-assisted information about insurance claims. It is not legal advice, and AI can be wrong. Information you receive should not be your only basis for important decisions.

• The information you share — including your contact details, claim facts, and uploaded documents — may be reviewed by ClaimGuard staff and partner law firms in your area for the purpose of evaluating whether they may be able to help with your claim.

• You consent to being contacted by ClaimGuard or its partner law firms by phone, text, or email regarding your claim. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.

• Use of ClaimGuard does not create an attorney-client relationship. An attorney-client relationship can only be formed by a written engagement signed by both you and a law firm.
